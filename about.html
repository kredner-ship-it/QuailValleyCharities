What Drives Us

Mission & vision

Our Mission

To strengthen Indian River County by funding nonprofit organizations that support children, education, and well-being — powered by the generosity of the Quail Valley Golf Club, River Club, and The Pointe community.

Our Vision

An Indian River County where every child has access to opportunity, every student has a path to education, and every family has the support it needs to thrive.

Our Values

How we make funding decisions

  • 01
    Local Impact — We fund organizations working directly in Indian River County, with a clear line to measurable outcomes.
  • 02
    Accountability — Grant recipients report on outcomes, and we report openly on how funds are raised and distributed.
  • 03
    Partnership — We fund and support nonprofits already doing excellent work, rather than duplicating their efforts.
  • 04
    Focus — We prioritize programs serving children, education, and community well-being.
