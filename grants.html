Funding Priorities

What we fund

Quail Valley Charities prioritizes grants to 501(c)(3) nonprofit organizations serving Indian River County in one or more of the following areas:

  • 01
    Children & Youth — mentorship, after-school care, and youth enrichment programs.
  • 02
    Education — scholarships, literacy, and academic support programs.
  • 03
    Well-Being — health, wellness, and family-support services.

Eligibility

Who can apply

Eligible Organizations

  • Hold current 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status
  • Directly serve residents of Indian River County
  • Align with our focus areas: children, education, or well-being
  • Demonstrate sound financial management and governance

Generally Not Funded

  • Organizations outside Indian River County
  • Individual sponsorships or scholarships to individuals
  • Political or religious activities
  • General endowment or capital campaign requests (placeholder — confirm policy)
